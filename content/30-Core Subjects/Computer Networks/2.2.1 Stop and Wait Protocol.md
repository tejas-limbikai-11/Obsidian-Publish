# Stop and Wait ARQ (Automatic Repeat reQuest) Protocol

The **Stop and Wait ARQ Protocol** is a **flow control and error-control protocol** typically used at the **[Data Link Layer](2.%20Data%20Link%20Layer.md)** but can also be implemented in the **[Transport Layer](4.%20Transport%20Layer.md)** for reliable data communication.

---

#### Working Mechanism

- **Frame Transmission**:
  - The sender transmits a single frame and **waits for an acknowledgment (ACK)** from the receiver before sending the next frame. This ensures each frame is delivered correctly before proceeding with the next.
  - If the acknowledgment is not received within a specific timeout period, the sender assumes the frame was lost or corrupted and **retransmits** it.

---

#### Sequence Numbers

- **Purpose of Sequence Numbers**:
  - Sequence numbers are used to uniquely identify each frame or packet, allowing the receiver to recognize and **differentiate between new frames and retransmitted frames**. This prevents errors, such as duplicate frames, if an acknowledgment is lost and the sender has to retransmit.
  - In Stop and Wait, sequence numbers are usually **0 and 1** (binary) since only two numbers are needed to differentiate between two consecutive frames.

---

#### Sender and Receiver Window Sizes

- **Sender Window Size**:
  - The **sender window size is 1**, meaning the sender can send only one frame at a time and must wait for an acknowledgment before sending the next frame.

- **Receiver Window Size**:
  - The **receiver window size is also 1**, allowing the receiver to process and acknowledge only one frame at a time. The receiver will only accept frames in sequence (the next expected frame) and discard any duplicate frames.

---

#### Summary

| **Property**             | **Description**                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Layer**                | Primarily used at the **[Data Link Layer](2.%20Data%20Link%20Layer%5C)**; can also be used at the **Transport Layer**. |
| **Transmission**         | Sends one frame at a time, waiting for acknowledgment before sending the next frame.                              |
| **Sequence Numbers**     | Typically **0 and 1**; used to differentiate consecutive frames and avoid duplicate data.                         |
| **Sender Window Size**   | **1** (only one outstanding frame is allowed).                                                                    |
| **Receiver Window Size** | **1** (only one frame can be processed at a time).                                                                |

---

The **Stop and Wait ARQ Protocol** is effective for low-throughput networks due to its simplicity. However, it can introduce delays in high-latency or high-throughput environments due to its single-frame waiting structure.
